Welcome to Sakura Cinema

Sakura Cinema is a special anime film season bringing some of Japan's best animated movies back to the big screen this November.

Whether you're a long-time anime fan or new to Japanese animation, there's something for everyone. From fantasy adventures and action to family stories, we've selected six films for you to enjoy.

Our November Film Season

This November, we're showing six Japanese animated films, including Princess Mononoke, Howl's Moving Castle, Wolf Children, Summer Wars, Sword of the Stranger and The Boy and the Beast.

Have a look through our movie selection to find out more about each film, including its story, screening times and ticket prices.
